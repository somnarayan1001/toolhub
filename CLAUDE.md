## Adding a tool

1. Decide with the owner whether it lives in a folder here, which makes its address `/toolhub/<tool>/`, or in its own repository.
2. Add a card to `index.html` and a line to `README.md`.
3. Check the page at 375 px wide, with no sideways scrolling.

The owner asks about a tool's purpose and audience first. Ask before anything that changes a public address, such as renaming a repository.
